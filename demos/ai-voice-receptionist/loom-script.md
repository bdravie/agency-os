# Loom script: AI voice receptionist (2 minutes)

**Set up before recording:** publish the workflow, open `site/index.html`, put your phone on screen (QuickTime > New Movie Recording > your iPhone) with the speaker on, and have the front desk phone (or n8n's Executions) ready to show.

## The problem (15s)
"This is an aesthetics clinic. The people who answer the phone are the same people doing the treatments, so most calls during the day ring out. And someone who fills in a contact form at nine at night usually hears nothing until the next morning, by which point they've booked somewhere else."

## The trigger (15s)
*Fill in the form with your own number and click Call me now.*
"So I'm a new client. I've left my number on the website at, say, nine at night."

## The automation running (60s)
*Your phone rings within seconds. Answer on speaker.*
"That's the clinic ringing me back already. It tells me straight away that it's the clinic's AI assistant and that the call is recorded."
*Have a natural conversation: first time, a consultation, Thursday after four. Ask it about a treatment, and let it say the practitioner will cover that at the consultation.*
"Notice it won't give treatment advice. It just takes the request."
*Hang up. Show the front desk's WhatsApp.*
"And the front desk has one line: Sam, new client, consultation, call Thursday after four, with the number."
*Optional: ring the clinic number to show that inbound calls work the same way.*

## The result (20s)
"Every call gets answered, every website enquiry gets a call in seconds, and the team only spend time on people who want to book."

## The offer (10s)
"If you want this answering your clinic's phone, let's talk it through. We'd start with a data processing agreement, because it handles your clients' details, and set it up around your diary and your team."
