How an introduction works

  1. We listen first

    What you do, who you serve, and what a good partner would add. No pitch decks at this stage.

  2. We make the introduction

    To one or two organisations whose aims genuinely overlap with yours, with a short note to both sides.

  3. We stay with both sides

    Through the first conversations and the agreement, until the partnership runs without us.

The monthly letter

One short email a month: partnerships that worked, companies looking for a cause, and events worth going to.
