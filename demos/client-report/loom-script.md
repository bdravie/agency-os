# Loom script: weekly client report (2 minutes)

**The problem (0:00-0:15)**
"Every Monday, somebody at your agency exports numbers from Google Ads and Meta, pastes them into a spreadsheet, works out what changed, and writes it up for the client. That's about three hours per client, every week."

**The trigger (0:15-0:30)**
Show the CSV (`sample-data/harbour_metrics.csv`): "This is the raw export, eight weeks of fake data for a made-up client. No customer data, just totals."

**The automation running (0:30-1:30)**
- Run the command in the terminal; it finishes in seconds.
- Open `report.html`. Point at the four headline tiles and the green and red arrows.
- Read one win and one concern aloud: "Google Ads is up, but Meta spend rose 25% while conversions fell 20%. It flagged that for you, and suggested what to check."
- Say: "All the maths is done in code, not by the AI, and every figure in the commentary is checked against the numbers."
- Open the PDF: "Same report, ready to send to the client."

**The result (1:30-1:50)**
"Three hours became about forty seconds, and it reads like an account manager wrote it. You review it and send it. Nothing goes out automatically."

**The offer (1:50-2:00)**
"I'll build this on one of your own clients' real data in 48 hours, read-only access, so you can see it before you commit. Reply and I'll set it up."
