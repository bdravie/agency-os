# Outreach email (agencies): edit freely

Send by hand from your own mailbox. Never automate. Generic company addresses only (info@, hello@, contact@, enquiries@). Limited companies only. Check the suppression list first.

## Rules (from the cold-email template)

- Subject under 30 characters, short and specific.
- Body under 100 words. The first 3 lines decide whether they reply.
- One genuine, specific opening line that you write yourself from their website. Never invent or copy a compliment.
- One small ask (15 minutes, or a free test on one client).
- Exactly one follow-up, 3-4 days later. Never more than two in total.
- Send Tuesday or Wednesday, around 12pm, 3pm or 5pm. Avoid Thursday and Friday.
- Always include your real name, business name and the opt-out line.

(The numbers behind these tips come from a Reddit summary about job-hunting emails, so treat them as guidance, not proof. Track your own replies.)

## First email

**Subject:** Weekly client reports

Hi {{agency}} team,

Your {{specific thing on their site, e.g. the case study for X}} caught my attention because {{genuine reason}}. I'm {{your name}} at {{your business}}: I build tools that write weekly ad reports for UK agencies. Here's a 2-minute demo: {{Loom link}}

Would 15 minutes this week be useful? I'd run it free on one of your clients' numbers, read-only access, so you can judge it yourself.

{{your name}}, {{your business}}
Reply "no" and I won't email again.

## Follow-up (once, 3-4 days later, same thread)

**Subject:** Re: Weekly client reports

Hi {{agency}} team, a quick nudge in case this got buried: {{Loom link}}. Happy to run it free on one client's numbers. If it's not relevant, reply "no" and I won't email again.

{{your name}}, {{your business}}

## Before you hit send

- [ ] Limited company, active, on Companies House
- [ ] Generic address on their own domain
- [ ] Not on the suppression list
- [ ] The opening line is true and specific to them
- [ ] Loom link works
