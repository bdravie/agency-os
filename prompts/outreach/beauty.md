# Outreach: Beauty studios

No outreach is drafted for this niche.

Beauty is an audience niche (docs/NICHE_FOCUS_PLAN.md): content on Instagram and TikTok and the 30-day content kit only.
Most lash, brow, hair and nail studios are sole traders, and cold email to a sole trader is not covered by the corporate-subscriber
basis the rest of the outreach relies on. The CRM refuses email drafts, sends, DMs and LinkedIn notes for this niche on the server
(niche status in config/niches.yaml). Reach these businesses through content instead.

A studio that is a clinic (a limited company, medically led or CQC-registered) belongs in the aesthetics niche.
